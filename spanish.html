A little daily practice

Five Spanish words a day

A fresh set of five common Castilian Spanish words, chosen automatically for today, each with a small picture cue. Tap the circle to hear each one pronounced — come back tomorrow for five new ones.

Sounds robotic? On Mac: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Manage Voices, then download an Enhanced or Premium Spanish voice.

How this works ›

The five words are picked automatically from a list of 100 common Castilian Spanish words, grouped into 20 fixed sets of 5, based on today's date — no server, no schedule, it just recalculates whenever the page loads. The same 20 sets repeat in order every 20 days.

Pronunciation uses your browser's built-in Spanish voice (Web Speech API), so quality depends on your browser and OS. The words themselves are Castilian Spanish; the voice your browser picks may lean Spain or Latin American depending on what's installed. If more than one Spanish voice is available, a picker appears above the words so you can choose whichever sounds best — your choice is remembered. On a Mac, System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices lets you download higher-quality "Premium" voices.

Tap the small dot next to a word to mark it as practiced today — that's saved locally in your browser, not shared anywhere.

Below the words, two short sentences combine most of that day's words together, using common everyday phrasing, so you can hear them used in context rather than in isolation.

The Review section uses spaced repetition: every word you've ever marked with the dot goes into your personal review pool. New words come back the next day; after that, saying "Got it" pushes the next review further out (1 day, then 6, then longer each time), while "Still learning" brings it back tomorrow. Everything is stored locally in your browser, going all the way back to the first word you marked.
