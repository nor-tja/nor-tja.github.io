A little daily practice

Five French words a day

A fresh set of five common French words, chosen automatically for today. Tap the circle to hear each one pronounced — come back tomorrow for five new ones.

How this works ›

The five words are picked automatically from a rotating list of 100 common French words, based on today's date — no server, no schedule, it just recalculates whenever the page loads. Each block of 5 stays fixed for the whole day, and the list reshuffles into a new order roughly every 20 days so you eventually see everything again in a different sequence.

Pronunciation uses your browser's built-in French voice (Web Speech API), so quality depends on your browser and OS. Chrome and Safari on Mac/iOS generally have good French voices built in.

Tap the small dot next to a word to mark it as practiced today — that's saved locally in your browser, not shared anywhere.
